Where do I find my files?

Strava: Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Request your archive. You get an email with a .zip: drop it here as is.

Garmin: Account → Data Management → Export Your Data. Drop the .zip you receive.

Nike Run Club: Nike has no export button. Its app data can be fetched with a small script (e.g. nrc-exporter); drop the folder of .json or .gpx files it creates.

Other apps: export your runs as GPX, TCX or FIT files and drop them here (several at once is fine).

Files are read in your browser: nothing is uploaded. A run starting within 2 minutes of one already on the map is skipped as a duplicate. Place names: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).
